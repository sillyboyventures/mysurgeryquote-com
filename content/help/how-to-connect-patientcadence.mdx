Connecting PatientCadence lets your team send quote PDFs to a patient's record in PatientCadence, and keeps PatientCadence updated when the patient opens the quote or pays a deposit.

**What You Can Do**

-   Send a quote PDF to a patient's Files in PatientCadence
-   Search PatientCadence patients by name, email or phone
-   Let PatientCadence know when a quote is sent, when the patient first opens it, and when a deposit is marked paid, with the dollar amount

**Before You Begin**

You will need:

-   A PatientCadence account for your practice
-   Your PatientCadence master admin, who makes the connection key
-   An admin role in MySurgeryQuote

**Step 1: Make a Connection Key in PatientCadence**

1.  Your PatientCadence master admin opens **Practice admin > Connections**.
2.  They confirm the practice has a signed Business Associate Agreement with MySurgeryQuote and make a key for MySurgeryQuote.
3.  PatientCadence shows the key once. It starts with **pcc_**. Copy it.

**Step 2: Connect in MySurgeryQuote**

1.  Go to **Admin → EMR Integration**.
2.  Choose **PatientCadence**.
3.  Paste the key into **Connection Key** and click **Connect to PatientCadence**. MySurgeryQuote checks the key with PatientCadence before saving it.
4.  You'll see "PatientCadence is connected".

**Step 3: Test the Connection**

1.  Click **Test Connection**.

**Sending a Quote**

1.  Open the quote and click **PatientCadence**.
2.  Search for the patient, select them, and send. The quote then shows as Sent.
3.  The first time the patient opens their quote link, PatientCadence adds it to their timeline and puts them on Needs you now so your team can follow up. When a deposit is marked paid, that goes to PatientCadence too.

**Troubleshooting**

-   **"Credentials rejected" or "PatientCadence connection key was rejected":** the key was turned off or replaced in PatientCadence. Ask your master admin for a new key, click **Disconnect**, and paste the new one.
-   **"No matching patients in PatientCadence":** MySurgeryQuote doesn't create patients in PatientCadence. Add the patient in PatientCadence first, then search again.
-   **Don't see the PatientCadence button on a quote:** only admins and users allowed to send to the EMR see it.
